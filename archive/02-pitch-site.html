The idea

The EP isn't a tracklist.
It's a map.

Every song becomes an island you can run through. The mood of the track is the weather, the landscape, and the thing chasing you. A boat ferries you between them, and a castle sits at the centre of the archipelago.

It lives as a tab inside the Lala Luna app, so the place fans go to hear from her is the same place they go to play. Each island unlocks when its single drops — the game becomes the rollout.

The archipelago

Four islands.
One hidden.

Island I

Green

4 Letters · the love song

Ruins, wild horses, a green dress and a white cowgirl hat. Golden hour turns to rain, and the rainbow comes after. Hand fans open on the sprint.

Island II

Good Riddance

the dinosaur island

Pure adventure. Overgrown, prehistoric, loud. The one that makes the clip people send each other.

Island III

Sanity

nighttime · ocean front

Lightning over black water. The full moon lands here — every other island shows a phase, this one shows the whole thing.

Island IV

Town Square

the Colombian beach

Glitter princess cow print, 1700s silhouettes cut modern. The social island — where the leaderboard lives and the secrets get compared.

The fifth island

LUNA

One letter is hidden on each island. Find all four in a single run of each and the bubble island surfaces — pop your way to the bottom and the unreleased track plays. Every secret found is posted to the leaderboard, so nobody finds it quietly.

The app

Somewhere to be a LUNA-tic.

Play

The game tab. Ferry, islands, leaderboard, secrets. Installs to the home screen like an app — no app store review, no 30% cut, ships the day you say go.

Hear from her

She broadcasts to everyone; fans reply into one filtered inbox she answers when she wants. Age-gated at signup, no fan-to-fan DMs.
