The 12 September findings, verified

Five claims came back from the sweep. Each was checked against the code and the running game before anything was changed — two held as stated, one was true but misdated, one was right with the wrong cause, and one did not reproduce at all.

01

Hub throws a setHex page error at index.html:5400

Confirmed · I was wrong

It fires 3× during traversal.mjs's ~1 hour hub run, and never in a short one. I tested for 36 seconds, saw four good lamps and no errors, and filed it not-reproduced — a window shorter than the one the report had already said comes back clean. The guard is now in: it logs once through DBG rather than killing the frame loop, so the next long run names the bad lamp. Root cause still unfound by either of us.

02

Camera clips into geometry on Hub and Good Riddance

Confirmed · cause differs

The arm collapses to its 3.5 floor when the arm sweeps a decor cylinder. A fix was attempted and reverted — refining the sample walk from 0.94 u to 0.03 u moved the number by 0.01. Now reproducible: on the seeded hub layout the strafe collapse is absent, but W-through-colliders still drops to 3.72 with 4 pops on both runs. The mechanism is intact and can finally be worked on; a different seed would likely bring the strafe case back too.

03

Every island carries two more lights than baseline

Not a bug · by design

The sweep corrected this itself, and it is right. LOOK defaults to "toon3d" (:483) and each island's setup then adds a bounce and a rim DirectionalLight by design (:4043-4050) — exactly +2, on every island, whatever the content, which is why the pattern was so uniform. I had attributed them to the desk candle and her lantern; those were in the baseline of four all along. Measured hub confirms it: Directional 0.38 #3c8cbe is the bounce, 0.42 #ffe6c1 the rim.

04

assets/lala.glb has grown to 6.65 MB

True · misdated

6,974,652 bytes, mtime 10 September — it is the v2 rebuild, two days before the run, not a change in this window. Every island pays for it and compression is still worth doing, but nothing regressed here.

05

HEAD_EAR and HEAD_CROWN are dead identifiers

True · fixed

Both unreferenced, and HEAD_SIDE with them — its own comment claimed it was "kept so the physics clamp still reads in the old names", and the clamp no longer uses it. All three removed; the measured constants they derive from are kept.

What the sweep did not find

The visual audit ran separately, through the real gameplay camera across all five islands. It turned up the thing that had been wrong for two weeks — and that no instrumented check was ever going to catch, because the measurement was pointed at the wrong object.

She was wearing the placeholder's hat

makeLala() builds a sketch figure to stand in until the GLB loads. attachRig() hides all twelve stand-in parts when the real rig lands — and then applyHeadwear() turned three of them straight back on, because it only ever asked is she bare-headed, never is the stand-in still the figure on screen.

What was rendering

1.339 u

A flat CylinderGeometry(.68,.68,.08) disc under a near-straight drum. The placeholder.

What was underneath it

0.748 u

The real cowgirlHat() — taco brim, cattleman crease, a pinch either side. There the whole time.

Measured live before the fix: 3 of 12 primitives visible on a fully rigged character, and they were brim, hatTop and band. One condition fixed it — const stand = !ch.model;. Every render of a too-big drum-shaped hat was a render of the placeholder, and so was every measurement made defending it.

The count was right; the conclusion was not. Of 374 unlit materials, 270 are translucent FX, 52 are emitters, 34 are sky — all correctly unlit. The real number is 18 opaque solids. Nothing about lighting is capped.
