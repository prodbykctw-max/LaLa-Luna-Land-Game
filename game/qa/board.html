Finding 01 — the setHex crash was never the castle lamps

The sweep read a fresh clone of remote HEAD and said the fix shipped on 12 September was never merged. It was half right, and the other half is worse: the fix was merged. It was pointed at the wrong object.

A guard is only a fix if it guards the thing that throws

holoMaterial() (index.html:3592) returns a THREE.ShaderMaterial and defines exactly one shim — opacity, forwarding to the uOpacity uniform — under a comment explaining that a ShaderMaterial has no such property. It has no .color either. Four lines below that comment, cueUpdate() runs a.userData.main.material.color.setHex(CUE_COL[mode]) on every jump↔glide cue change. That is the throw. It only fires on a mode change, which is why an hour of traversal.mjs sees it three times and a 36-second run never does.

The guard shipped on 2026-09-12c was on I.castleLamps — a different object, which was never throwing. It was written without ever reproducing the crash, and the board then described it in the past tense.

Without the shim

throws

material.color is false; .setHex() raises Cannot read properties of undefined; uTint unchanged.

With the shim

returns

material.color is true; the call returns; uTint moves to the target colour.

Proven both directions by qa/cuecrash.mjs (new) — same island, same build, ± the one block. The fix mirrors the opacity shim: .color forwards into uTint. The recolour had never worked either — even arrows that didn't throw never changed colour between jump and glide.

Finding 02 — momentum was swinging the camera off geometry

Verified here independently before touching it, on the shipped build, then again after the fix.

01

Holding W in a straight line swung the camera a quarter turn because she scraped a rock

Confirmed · fixed · live

2026-09-14a made heading chase atan2(W.vx, W.vz) — actual velocity. tryMove() zeroes the blocked axis on a collision, so the next frame's velocity is the slid vector; heading chased it and the always-behind camera chased heading. Measured on W-through-colliders: green 28.5°, gr 23.7° against flat 0° in every prior report. Heading now aims at the stick while she is being driven and falls back to velocity only when coasting — the body still slides, still carries speed, still deflects; only the facing stops being a function of whatever she's brushing. After: 0° on every segment of all five islands.

02

GR's plain A/D distance pops — same commit, different mechanism, not fixed

Open · not claimed

The sweep flagged 5–8 pops on segments that read flat 0 before momentum; measured here at 7 and 5. After the heading fix they are 6 and 6 — unmoved, as expected, because they are the camera arm collapsing to its 3.5 floor rather than anything to do with facing. That is the camera-arm-collapse item already tracked, now with a second reproducible trigger.
